# SydMap: the map page

Scope: web/index.html, the whole viewer. Mode: Operate, with the city as the experience. Visitor: people curious about
Sydney's buildings and history, from a shared link or thethirdscript.com. Owner's ask (2026-10-14): its own style and
character in menus, layout, wording and the notes, not a recoloured LAYRS: Amsterdam. Opening: straight to the map.
Names: Ages, Stories, Heights, Heritage, Walks, Notes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Sydney read along one line through time. Time is the spine of the page: the legend and the timeline are one
rail of ten era stations along the bottom, with the logo's sun riding it as the year. Refuses the LAYRS arrangement of
a centred mode pill, a big year top right and a legend sheet on the left.

OWN-WORLD: The Third Script's wall, stone land, cypress-teal harbour, charcoal ink and terracotta sun; Cormorant
Garamond for names, years and eras, Inter for information. Underlined text tabs, a right-hand reading dock on paper,
station rings on a 4px era rail, year digits on paper flaps.

STORY: The visitor sees the city today, scrubs or plays the line to watch it grow, taps a station to see one era,
switches lens to heights, heritage or a story, opens a building in the dock to read how its year is known, and walks a
route of stops.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Top left "Sydney" with the sun behind its "y", SydMap and the studio's mark beneath. Top right, tabs
Ages, Stories, Heights, Heritage, then Walks, Notes and a "Find a building or street" field. The map fills the rest. Bottom
left, the year on four paper flaps with the era in teal italic and the count beside it; under it the play button and the
rail with ten stations, the histogram above it, the sun at today. The dock opens on the right only when there is
something to read.

FORM: grounded candidate 6 of 7, the dealt lead (structure: the line through time), seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance

## Signature

The rail: era stations as the legend and the sun as the year handle; digits flip when the year changes.
