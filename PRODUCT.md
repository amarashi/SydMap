## Platform

web

## Users

People curious about Sydney: residents, visitors, students and teachers, and anyone who enjoys maps and local
history. They arrive from a shared link, from The Third Script's site (thethirdscript.com, where SydMap is shown as
the studio's work), or from GitHub. They want to see how the city grew and what stands where, and to play.
(Inferred from the README and the page; not confirmed with the owner.)

## Product Purpose

SydMap shows every building in the City of Sydney and North Sydney in 3D, coloured by the year it was built and set
in the history that shaped it. A visitor can drag the timeline or play the city's growth, colour the city by period,
storeys, heritage or context layers, open any building's card with the evidence for its year, play quests, and open
every building's data to download. It is a non-commercial project, made out of curiosity and for education, and a
piece of work by The Third Script, the Sydney studio of Amir Marashi.

## Brand Commitments

- The look is The Third Script's: its logo's charcoal, cypress teal and terracotta sun, Cormorant Garamond and Inter.
  See DESIGN.md.
- The original idea is credited wherever the project is described: a Sydney rebuild of LAYRS: Amsterdam by Alex
  Barakov (MIT), itself inspired by Alexander Bogachev's Paris, building by building.
- The Acknowledgement of Country stays on the page.
- Australian English, short plain sentences, sources named (CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Product Principles

1. The city leads; the interface is its label.
2. Every year shows its evidence; estimates are marked as estimates.
3. Name every source and licence.
4. Do not make persecution or loss into a game.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard focus is visible (cypress teal), text meets WCAG AA on white, colours are never the only code (the legend,
the timeline and the card name each period), and reduced motion turns off transitions and the quests' stamp.
