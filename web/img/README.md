# Images

From The Third Script's approved logo package (`The_Third_Script_Logo_Package/` in the thethirdscript repository),
© The Third Script, used here because SydMap is the studio's own work:

- `sun.webp`: the logo's sun texture (the site's `src/img/sun.webp`), resized to 160 px. SydMap tints it terracotta
  for its mark, the play button and nothing else.
- `the-third-script-symbol.webp`: `icon_512x512_transparent.png` (the cypress trees and the sun), cropped to the
  artwork and resized to 96 px tall.
- `the-third-script-wordmark.webp`: `04_wordmark_only_transparent.png`, resized to 40 px tall.

`docs/the-third-script.png` (the README's lockup) is `05_horizontal_lockup_transparent.png`, cropped and resized to
120 px tall. `web/og.png` and `docs/screenshots/` are screenshots of the page itself (Chromium, 1200 × 630 and
1280 × 800, the phone at 390 × 844).
